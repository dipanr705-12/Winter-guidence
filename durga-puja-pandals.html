🚶 Recommended Pandal Hopping Routes

🏛️ Trail 1: North Kolkata Heritage Walk

Best for rich traditional culture, narrow historic lanes, and artistic craftsmanship.

  • 1️⃣ Sovabazar Rajbari (Metro: Sovabazar)
  • 2️⃣ Kumartuli Park & Artisans Colony
  • 3️⃣ Ahiritola Sarbojanin
  • 4️⃣ Bagbazar Sarbojanin Ghat
  • 5️⃣ College Square & Md. Ali Park

🎨 Trail 2: South Kolkata Metro Sprint

Best for avant-garde theme designs, lighting spectacles, and grand art installations.

  • 1️⃣ Suruchi Sangha (New Alipore)
  • 2️⃣ Chetla Agrani (Metro: Kalighat)
  • 3️⃣ Deshapriya Park & Tridhara Sammilani
  • 4️⃣ Ekdalia Evergreen & Singhi Park
  • 5️⃣ Mudiali Club & Shib Mandir

✨ Trail 3: VIP Road & Salt Lake Circuit

Famous for colossal palace/monument replicas and massive light displays.

  • 1️⃣ Sree Bhumi Sporting Club (VIP Road)
  • 2️⃣ Lake Town Adhibasi Brinda
  • 3️⃣ Dum Dum Park Bharat Chakra
  • 4️⃣ Dum Dum Park Tarun Sangha
  • 5️⃣ Salt Lake FD & BJ Block

🚅 Trail 4: District Mega Wonders

Iconic pandals in neighboring districts that draw millions every year.

  • 1️⃣ Kalyani ITI More (Luminous Park, Nadia)
  • 2️⃣ Barasat Sandhani Club (North 24 Pgs)
  • 3️⃣ Chandannagar Strand (Hooghly Lighting)
  • 4️⃣ Shibpur Mandirtala (Howrah)
  • 5️⃣ Dadabhai Sporting Club (Siliguri)

ℹ️ Important Traveler Tips & Emergency Helplines

🚇 All-Night Kolkata Metro

Blue Line (Dakshineswar - Kavi Subhash) runs special night trains on Saptami, Ashtami, and Navami, running past 4:00 AM.

🕒 Ideal Timing

10 AM – 3 PM: Easy walking, minimal queues, great for photography.
6 PM – 4 AM: Full carnival energy and breathtaking illumination.

🚨 Kolkata Police Helpline

Dial 100 / 112 for emergency. Women helpline: 1091. Kolkata Traffic Control: 033-2214-3644.

👟 Pandal Hopping Essentials

Wear comfortable walking shoes, carry a water bottle, keep loose cash or UPI ready, and save offline transit maps on your phone.
